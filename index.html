About Us

Our Story

NAMI — meaning "wave" in Georgian (ნამი) — was born from a simple idea: bring the precision and purity of Japanese sushi to the heart of our city, served with the generosity and warmth Georgians are known for.

Our chefs train in traditional knife technique and rice preparation, while our produce is sourced daily from local markets and trusted seafood suppliers. The result is a menu that honors tradition while feeling completely at home here.

Whether you're joining us for a quiet lunch, a celebration, or a late-night roll with friends, our goal is the same every time: fresh food, honest hospitality, no rush.
